How to play

Drag from one of your star systems to any other to send ships. Fleets travel only along hyperlanes and fight any system they pass that you don't own. Forges build ships, Citadels defend at 1.6× strength, and Nexus systems generate research. From the third minute, stars start going supernova. The in-game How to Play screen covers every rule.

Mac controls

Space pauses. F toggles 2× speed. Keys 1 to 4 set how many ships you send. 0 re-centres the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag empty space to pan.

Common questions

Where is my campaign progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does Hyperlane need an internet connection? No. It works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.
