Privacy Policy

Hyperlane: Galactic Conquest · Effective 24 October 2026

Summary

Hyperlane does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your campaign progress and Quick Match settings on your device, using the standard system preferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer. Deleting the app removes it.

Network access

Hyperlane does not connect to the internet. It contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising code.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
